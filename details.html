What readers can expect

This is a playful story rather than a formal lesson. It uses an unusual name, familiar experiences, and comic disorder to create a small world that rewards curiosity.

The story is designed to be enjoyable when read independently or shared aloud. The wordplay gives adults something to notice while the characters and situations remain accessible to younger readers.
