Ella Umber & Her Umber-Ella

A playful, laugh-out-loud story filled with wordplay, weather, and one very messy dog.

Ella Umber lives in a world where ordinary moments can become wonderfully strange. Add a dog, a little weather, and a name that refuses to behave normally, and the result is a story built for readers who enjoy imagination, humor, and the joy of language.
