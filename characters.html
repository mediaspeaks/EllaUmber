Meet the Characters

A girl, her dog, and a very special umbrella.
Ella Umber ♥
Curious. Kind. Imaginative. Always ready for an adventure.

Ella Umber is a bright and curious girl who sees the world a little differently. She notices the small things, asks the big questions, and turns ordinary days into something special.

Ella loves exploring, collecting ideas, and finding joy in everyday moments. Whether it is a sunny day, a rainy day, or a day full of surprises, Ella is always ready, especially when she has her Umber-Ella and Biscuit by her side.

“Big curiosity leads to bright adventures.”Ella
Biscuit 🐾
Loyal. Playful. A little mischievous. Always with Ella.

Biscuit is Ella's best friend and constant companion. He's a happy, adventurous pup with a big personality and an even bigger heart.

Biscuit is always ready to explore, chase a stick, splash in a puddle, or curl up for a quiet moment. He brings fun, laughter, and unconditional support to every adventure. No matter what the day brings, Biscuit is right there, tail wagging.

“Puddles are better together.”Biscuit
The Umber-Ella ☂
More than an umbrella, a companion for every kind of day.

The Umber-Ella is no ordinary umbrella. It keeps Ella dry in the rain, shaded in the sun, and company on all of life's in-between days.

It represents imagination, comfort, and the magic of seeing possibility where others see only weather. With Ella, the Umber-Ella turns everyday moments into adventures.

“Every day has a brighter side.”The Umber-Ella
